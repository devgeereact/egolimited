The complete EGO LTD email system — every outreach and lifecycle sequence recomposed as comprehensive, **company-voice** emails (sent as EGO LTD / "the EGO team", never a personal name). This supersedes the earlier drafts in `11 — Email Sequences` and the email parts of `17 — Outreach Messages`: same brand voice (plain British, proof-led, "here's the receipt, not trust me"), but fuller bodies, subject-line options, preview text, timing, branching and benchmarks — ready to build in the app's bulk-email **campaigns** feature.

## What's inside

- **Part 1 — B2B sequences** (the growth prize): cold outreach to letting/managing agents, landlords, and commercial/facilities, plus an account nurture-and-retention cycle for clients who've used us once.
- **Part 2 — Household sequences**: homeowner enquiry-to-win, post-job review + referral, seasonal campaigns, and win-back.
- **Global reference**: performance benchmarks, flow diagrams, A/B tests, metrics, and a build checklist.

## Ground rules (apply to every email)

- **Company voice.** Sender is EGO LTD / the EGO team / "we". No first-person "I", no personal name. Reads as a real firm run by real people, not a corporation.
- **One clear ask per email.** Usually: reply, call **07539 705576**, or book a short call.
- **Payment is bank transfer only. Notifications are email only.** Never promise SMS or card payment.
- **Real proof only.** A made-up figure or review loses an account faster than no proof. If a `{{token}}` is blank, rewrite the line so it reads naturally — never send a visible `{{token}}`.
- **Stop the moment they convert.** Never let a "still thinking?" or a follow-up land after someone's said yes.
- **Compliance.** Cold B2B business email carries a one-line opt-out and a suppression list. Don't cold-email private households — domestic sequences are for people who enquired, are existing customers, or opted in (UK PECR/GDPR).
