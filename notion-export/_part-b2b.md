Comprehensive, company-voice B2B email sequences for EGO LTD — written to be sent through the app's bulk-email **campaigns** feature. Every email speaks as "the EGO team", leads with the receipt (per-property maintenance record, live tokenised job-tracking link, itemised VAT invoices, fixed price in writing), and never promises SMS or card payment. Swap the `{{tokens}}` for real values before sending, and only ever quote real jobs, real figures and real reviews. Stop any sequence the moment a contact replies, books, or accepts.

## How to use these sequences

- **Company voice, not personal.** The sender is EGO LTD / the EGO team / "we". No first-person "I", no personal name.
- **One clear ask per email.** Usually: reply, call **07539 705576**, or book a short call.
- **Cold B2B only.** These four sequences are for businesses (agents, landlords, commercial/facilities). Every cold email carries a one-line opt-out. Keep a suppression list and honour opt-outs immediately.
- **Payment is bank transfer only. Notifications are email only.** Never promise SMS or card payment.
- **Real proof only.** A made-up figure or review loses a B2B account faster than no proof at all. If a `{{token}}` is blank, rewrite the line so it still reads naturally — never send a visible `{{token}}` or an empty greeting.
- **Send weekday mornings** (Tue–Thu, 8–10am) for B2B. One sequence per contact at a time. Quiet anyone who's mid-job.

**Standard sign-off block — use on every email:**

```
The EGO LTD team
07539 705576 · hello@egolimited.com · egolimited.com
General maintenance, homes & commercial — South-East London
```
