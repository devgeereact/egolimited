A working bank of marketing tactics for EGO LTD — a South-East London maintenance firm chasing recurring B2B work (landlords, letting agents, managing agents, commercial/facilities) on the back of a proper per-property maintenance record, live job-tracking links, and fixed prices in writing. Each idea has an effort rating, the impact you can realistically expect, and a rough cost. Nothing here is a SaaS growth hack; it's all stuff a four-trade maintenance company in SE13 can actually do. Work top-down — the "Top 10 to do first" table at the bottom is the order I'd run them in.

## B2B acquisition — letting agents, managing agents, landlords

This is the prize. One signed managing agent can be worth more than fifty one-off domestic jobs, and the app is built to win exactly these accounts.

- **Walk-in the high-street agents.** Physically visit every lettings/estate agent within a 2-mile radius of SE13 (Lewisham, Blackheath, Catford, New Cross, Greenwich) with a one-page leave-behind and a business card. Ask who handles their maintenance now. **Effort: Med · Impact: High · Cost: Low** (printing + shoe leather).
- **The "approved contractor" pitch.** Offer to go on an agent's contractor panel: fixed-price written quotes, VAT invoices, your tracking link so they can see job status without phoning you. Agents live in fear of no-shows — lead with reliability, not price. **Effort: Med · Impact: High · Cost: Low.**
- **Property manager LinkedIn outreach.** Find property/block managers and lettings directors in SE/South London on LinkedIn, send a short personal note (not a pitch deck) offering to take their reactive repairs off their plate. **Effort: Med · Impact: Med · Cost: Low.**
- **Free first-job trial for new agents.** "Give us one repair this month — no tie-in. See the tracking link and the invoice, then decide." Removes the risk of switching contractor. **Effort: Low · Impact: High · Cost: Med** (one subsidised job).
- **Void-turnaround package.** Sell agents a fixed-price "between-tenancy" bundle: touch-up decorating, minor plumbing checks, re-seal bathrooms, fix the snags that lose deposits. Voids are predictable, recurring revenue. **Effort: Med · Impact: High · Cost: Low.**
- **End-of-tenancy snag list service.** Offer to attend check-out with the agent and quote the whole snag list on the spot in writing. Saves them chasing three trades. **Effort: Low · Impact: Med · Cost: Low.**
- **Block management cold list.** Build a list of SE London block/estate management companies (Companies House + agent directories) and approach them for communal repairs and planned upkeep. **Effort: Med · Impact: High · Cost: Low.**
- **Landlord forum + Facebook group presence.** Join SE London / National Landlords-type groups, answer questions genuinely (no spam), be the helpful local trades name people remember. **Effort: Low · Impact: Med · Cost: Free.**
- **"Switch your contractor" comparison sheet.** A simple one-pager showing what an agent gets with EGO vs a typical sole trader: written fixed price, VAT invoice, live tracking, per-property history. **Effort: Low · Impact: Med · Cost: Low.**
- **Quarterly business review for key accounts.** Once you've landed an agent, send them a short quarterly summary of every job done across their portfolio (the app already has the data). Makes you impossible to drop. **Effort: Low · Impact: High · Cost: Free.**
- **Target small commercial/FM directly.** Cafés, salons, dentists, small offices, nurseries in SE13/SE6 — they all need reactive maintenance and rarely have a contract. Door-to-door or email. **Effort: Med · Impact: Med · Cost: Low.**

## The app as a marketing asset

The custom app is a genuine differentiator that sole traders and most local firms can't match. Sell the experience, not the software.

- **Make the tracking link the hero of every pitch.** Show agents/landlords a live demo job tracking link on your phone in the meeting. "You'll always know where the job's at without ringing me." This alone closes B2B deals. **Effort: Low · Impact: High · Cost: Free.**
- **Per-property maintenance record as the lock-in.** Pitch landlords on a permanent history of everything done at their property — great for compliance, selling, and not paying twice to diagnose the same fault. Switching away means losing the record. **Effort: Low · Impact: High · Cost: Free.**
- **Branded tracking-link screenshots in marketing.** Use clean screenshots of the tracking link in the Stone & Ember brand on the website, leave-behinds and social. Proof you're more organised than the competition. **Effort: Low · Impact: Med · Cost: Free.**
- **Reviews engine on autopilot.** Trigger a review request automatically when a job is marked complete in the app. Steady drip of fresh Google reviews with zero manual chasing. **Effort: Low · Impact: High · Cost: Free.**
- **Itemised VAT invoice as a selling point.** Many landlords need clean VAT invoices for their accountant/HMRC. Make this explicit in B2B copy — it's a real reason to choose you over a cash-job sole trader. **Effort: Low · Impact: Med · Cost: Free.**
- **"Fixed price in writing, before we start" guarantee.** Bake the written-quote promise into all messaging. Removes the single biggest fear customers have about tradesmen. **Effort: Low · Impact: High · Cost: Free.**
- **Customer portal as a retention tool.** Give repeat domestic customers a simple way to see their history and re-book. Stickier than a phone number in someone's contacts. **Effort: Med · Impact: Med · Cost: Free.**
- **Short demo video of the tracking experience.** 30–60 sec phone video walking through booking → tracking link → completion → invoice. Use on the homepage and in agent emails. **Effort: Med · Impact: Med · Cost: Low.**

## Reviews & reputation

For a local trades firm, reviews are the single biggest conversion lever. Aim to be the most-reviewed maintenance firm in Lewisham.

- **Google Business Profile, fully filled in.** Claim and complete the GBP: services, area served, photos, hours, the SE13 address. This is the #1 local lead source and it's free. **Effort: Low · Impact: High · Cost: Free.**
- **Ask for the review at the right moment.** The minute a customer says "that's great, thanks" — that's when the app should fire the request. Add a polite personal nudge for big jobs. **Effort: Low · Impact: High · Cost: Free.**
- **Reply to every review, good or bad.** Shows you're attentive and human. Calm, professional replies to any negative one reassure future readers more than the complaint worries them. **Effort: Low · Impact: Med · Cost: Free.**
- **Before/after photos with every job.** Engineers snap before/after on completion (app already stores photos). Use the best ones publicly — visual proof beats words. **Effort: Low · Impact: High · Cost: Free.**
- **Checkatrade / MyBuilder / Rated People listing.** Get listed on at least one trades platform for domestic lead flow and third-party-verified reviews. Pick one, work it hard. **Effort: Med · Impact: Med · Cost: Med** (subscription/leads).
- **Trustpilot or Google review wall on the site.** Pull your best reviews onto the website. Social proof on the page where people decide. **Effort: Low · Impact: Med · Cost: Free.**
- **Case studies of recurring B2B accounts.** Once you have a happy agent, write a short "how we handle their reactive repairs" story (with permission). Sells the next agent. **Effort: Med · Impact: Med · Cost: Free.**

## Content & local SEO

You don't need a blog mill. You need to rank for "maintenance/handyman/decorator near me" across SE London and answer the questions people actually search.

- **Local landing pages per area + trade.** Pages like "Painter & decorator in Blackheath", "Minor plumbing repairs Lewisham", "Tiling Catford". Ranks for the searches that convert. **Effort: Med · Impact: High · Cost: Low.**
- **Nail the GBP categories and service list.** Map every trade (carpentry, decorating, plumbing, tiling) to GBP services so you appear for each search. **Effort: Low · Impact: High · Cost: Free.**
- **"How much does X cost in London" guides.** Honest price-range guides (e.g. "what does it cost to re-tile a small bathroom in London?"). Builds trust and pulls search traffic. **Effort: Med · Impact: Med · Cost: Low.**
- **Seasonal how-to / when-to-call posts.** "Spotting damp before winter", "should you patch or repaint?" — short, useful, locally framed. **Effort: Med · Impact: Med · Cost: Low.**
- **Local backlinks & citations.** Get listed in SE London directories, the Lewisham business listings, Yell, Bing Places, FreeIndex — consistent name/address/phone everywhere. **Effort: Med · Impact: Med · Cost: Free.**
- **FAQ page that answers real objections.** "Do you give fixed prices?", "Are you VAT registered?", "How fast can you come out?", "Do you do small jobs?" Answers = trust + SEO. **Effort: Low · Impact: Med · Cost: Free.**
- **A genuine "small jobs others won't book" page.** This is a real gap in the market and a distinct search intent. Own it. **Effort: Low · Impact: Med · Cost: Free.**

## Referral & word-of-mouth

Maintenance is a referral business. Make referring you effortless and slightly rewarding.

- **Agent referral incentive.** A small thank-you (gift voucher / fee credit) when one agent introduces you to another branch or another agency. **Effort: Low · Impact: High · Cost: Low.**
- **Domestic "recommend a neighbour" offer.** £20 off their next job (or the neighbour's first) for a successful referral. SE London terraces talk to each other. **Effort: Low · Impact: Med · Cost: Low.**
- **Always leave two cards.** Engineers leave two business cards on every job — "one for you, one for a neighbour." Costs nothing, compounds. **Effort: Low · Impact: Med · Cost: Free.**
- **Thank-you-for-the-referral note.** Whenever someone refers you, send a quick handwritten/email thank-you. People refer again when they feel noticed. **Effort: Low · Impact: Med · Cost: Free.**
- **Reciprocal referrals with non-competing trades.** Swap referrals with a local gas-safe engineer, electrician, roofer, locksmith — trades you don't do but customers ask for. **Effort: Low · Impact: Med · Cost: Free.**

## Partnerships

Borrow other people's customer bases instead of buying your own.

- **Estate agents (sales side).** Sales agents constantly need pre-sale touch-ups and snag fixes for vendors. Different contact from lettings — pitch both. **Effort: Med · Impact: High · Cost: Low.**
- **Local accountants & mortgage brokers.** They advise landlords. Get on their "trusted trades" recommendation list. **Effort: Low · Impact: Med · Cost: Free.**
- **Interior designers & small builders.** Designers need reliable hands for finishing work; builders sub out small snags. Be their go-to. **Effort: Low · Impact: Med · Cost: Free.**
- **Local hardware/builders' merchants.** A card on the counter at the SE13 merchants, maybe a mutual recommend. **Effort: Low · Impact: Low · Cost: Free.**
- **Office-cleaning & FM companies.** They get asked for repairs they don't do — become their maintenance partner. **Effort: Med · Impact: Med · Cost: Low.**
- **Insurance loss adjusters / claims handlers.** Local property claims need trades who quote properly and invoice cleanly. A steady, well-paid niche. **Effort: Med · Impact: Med · Cost: Low.**

## Retention & upsell of recurring contracts

It's far cheaper to keep an account than win one. The recurring/planned-upkeep engine is the whole business model — push it.

- **Annual maintenance plan for landlords.** Fixed monthly fee covering periodic checks + priority call-out + discounted hours. Turns one-off customers into recurring revenue. **Effort: Med · Impact: High · Cost: Low.**
- **Planned upkeep calendar per property.** Use the app's recurring plans to schedule seasonal checks automatically and remind the landlord. Sells the next visit before they ask. **Effort: Low · Impact: High · Cost: Free.**
- **"You're due" reminders.** Automated nudge when a property is due its next decorating refresh / re-seal / check. Re-books dormant customers. **Effort: Low · Impact: Med · Cost: Free.**
- **Post-job upsell, gently.** When closing a job, note the obvious next thing ("the hallway could do with a repaint") and quote it in writing. Soft, helpful, lucrative. **Effort: Low · Impact: Med · Cost: Free.**
- **Win-back lapsed accounts.** Once a quarter, email customers you've not seen in 6–12 months with a small reason to return. **Effort: Low · Impact: Med · Cost: Low.**
- **Multi-property volume pricing.** Offer agents/landlords better rates across a whole portfolio in exchange for being the default contractor. Locks in volume. **Effort: Low · Impact: High · Cost: Low.**

## Low-cost guerrilla & local

Cheap, physical, hyper-local. This is where a small budget goes furthest in SE13.

- **Van as a billboard.** Clean Stone & Ember livery on the van: name, trades, phone, "fixed prices in writing", website. Parked in SE13 it markets all day for free. **Effort: Low · Impact: High · Cost: Med** (one-off wrap).
- **A-board / yard sign on active jobs.** A tasteful "EGO at work here" sign outside a job pulls neighbours. **Effort: Low · Impact: Med · Cost: Low.**
- **Leaflet the streets around every completed job.** Drop 30–50 leaflets near a fresh job — "we just did work at no. 42, here's our card." Hyper-relevant, high response. **Effort: Med · Impact: Med · Cost: Low.**
- **Cards on community noticeboards.** Cafés, libraries, gyms, churches, community centres across Lewisham/Blackheath. **Effort: Low · Impact: Low · Cost: Free.**
- **Local Facebook "recommendations" replies.** When someone in an SE London group asks for a trade, be the prompt, polite reply (or get a happy customer to tag you). **Effort: Low · Impact: Med · Cost: Free.**
- **Nextdoor presence.** Maintenance recommendations spread fast on Nextdoor in residential SE London. Be active and helpful. **Effort: Low · Impact: Med · Cost: Free.**
- **Sponsor something tiny and local.** A kids' football team, a school fair, a community event — small spend, real local goodwill and a banner with your name. **Effort: Low · Impact: Low · Cost: Low.**
- **Smart, branded workwear.** Branded polos/hi-vis. A tidy uniformed person on a doorstep reassures and gets remembered. **Effort: Low · Impact: Med · Cost: Low.**

## Seasonal (UK calendar)

Tie outreach to what's actually breaking that month. Pre-book before the rush.

- **Autumn/winter — damp, leaks, heating-adjacent repairs.** Push "winter-proof your property" checks Sept–Nov: damp spotting, sealing, draught fixes, leak repairs before the cold. **Effort: Med · Impact: High · Cost: Low.**
- **Winter — void season for agents.** Tenancies turn over around year-end; pitch agents your void-turnaround package in Nov–Jan. **Effort: Low · Impact: High · Cost: Low.**
- **Spring — decorating season.** Most-booked time for interior/exterior painting. Run a spring decorating push Feb–Apr with fixed-price refresh packages. **Effort: Med · Impact: High · Cost: Low.**
- **Spring — exterior & woodwork.** Better weather = exterior painting, fascias, garden joinery, fence repairs. Promote while it's dry. **Effort: Low · Impact: Med · Cost: Low.**
- **Summer — bigger disruptive jobs.** Tenants on holiday / families away = good window for tiling, full repaints, bathroom re-seals. Pitch landlords to schedule now. **Effort: Low · Impact: Med · Cost: Low.**
- **Pre-Christmas — "get it done before guests".** Domestic push late Nov: small jobs, touch-ups, the niggles people want sorted before family visit. **Effort: Low · Impact: Med · Cost: Low.**
- **New year — annual plan sign-ups.** January is when landlords sort the year out. Promote the annual maintenance plan hard. **Effort: Low · Impact: High · Cost: Low.**
- **Post-storm rapid response.** After named storms, email/post offering quick repairs for storm damage (loose fixings, leaks, broken fences). Timely = high response. **Effort: Low · Impact: Med · Cost: Free.**
