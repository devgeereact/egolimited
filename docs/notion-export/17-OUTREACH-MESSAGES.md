A ready-to-send message bank for sourcing new clients — B2B (letting/managing agents, landlords, commercial & facilities) and households (domestic). Every message is short, plain-British and proof-led: lead with the receipt (per-property record, live tracking link, itemised VAT invoice, fixed price in writing), never "trust me". Swap the `{{tokens}}` before sending. Stop any sequence the moment they reply, book, or accept.

## How to use this

- **Channels covered:** Email · WhatsApp/text · LinkedIn · phone-call script + voicemail · in-person leave-behind follow-up.
- **One ask per message.** The ask is usually: reply, call **07539 705576**, a 10–15 min chat, or a trial job.
- **Golden rule:** never send a "just checking in" that lands after they've said yes. Quiet anyone mid-job.
- **Keep it real.** Only quote real jobs, real figures, real reviews. Made-up proof loses a B2B account faster than no proof.
- **Cadence:** B2B — space touches over ~3 weeks (Day 0, 4, 9, 15, 22). Household — Day 0, then +3 days, then +7. Send B2B weekday mornings (Tue–Thu, 8–10am).

**Default sign-off**

Cheers,

EGO LTD — general maintenance, homes & commercial

# PART D — The proof lines (drop into any message)

- "Fixed price agreed in writing before we start — no surprises on the bill."
- "You get a live link to follow the job — no waiting in all day, no chasing."

- "We keep a record per property, so the next job goes even quicker."
- "One number for the lot — carpentry, decorating, plumbing, tiling."

- The line: **The jobs that need doing, done properly.**
- The wedge: **Per-property record, live job tracking, itemised VAT invoices, fixed price in writing. Here's the receipt — not "trust me".**
