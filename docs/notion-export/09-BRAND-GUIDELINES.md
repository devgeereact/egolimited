These are the brand guidelines for EGO LTD — the rules that keep everything we put out, from a van decal to the app dashboard, looking and sounding like one company. The brand system is called **Stone & Ember**: warm, grounded stone tones doing the everyday work, with a single scarce ember-orange that does all the shouting. If you only remember one thing, remember that: ember is rare on purpose. Read this before you design a screen, lay out a flyer, write a sign-off, or brief anyone external.

## Brand Essence & Positioning

**EGO LTD** is a general maintenance company working across homes and commercial properties in South-East London. We do the unglamorous, essential work — repairs, upkeep, the jobs other people put off — and we do it properly, on time, tidied up after.

Our positioning is **premium but grounded**. We are not the cheapest van on the street and we are not a faceless national chain. We are the trusted local trade you'd recommend to your mum: skilled, reliable, respectful of your home, straight with you about cost and timing. Everything in this brand should feel **trustworthy, calm, and competent** — never loud, never salesy, never cutting corners.

**The essence in one line:** quiet confidence. Real work, done right, no fuss.

**Positioning statement:** For homeowners and property managers in South-East London who need maintenance done properly the first time, EGO LTD is the dependable general-maintenance team that turns up, does the work to a high standard, and leaves the place tidy — combining the care of a local tradesperson with the systems and accountability of a professional outfit.

## The Name

- **Legal / full name:** EGO LIMITED
- **Working brand name:** **EGO LTD** — this is the form used almost everywhere (logo, web, sign-off, marketing).
- **Spoken / shorthand:** "EGO"

Rules:
- Always set the name in **capitals**: EGO LTD. Never "Ego Ltd", "ego", or "EGO Ltd."
- Use **EGO LIMITED** only where a full legal entity name is required (contracts, invoices' legal footer, official registration). Everywhere else, **EGO LTD**.
- In the logo lockup, "LTD" is tinted ember while "EGO" stays in the foreground colour — see Logo Usage. In plain running text, do not try to recreate that colour split; just write **EGO LTD** in one colour.
- Never translate, abbreviate to "EL", or add descriptors into the name itself (it's not "EGO Maintenance Ltd").

## Tagline

**Primary tagline:**

> **The jobs that need doing, done properly.**

This is the line. It captures the whole positioning — the everyday work, done to a standard. Lead with it on the homepage, the van, and the first line of marketing.

**Approved short variants** (use when space is tight, e.g. social bios, sign-writing, email footers):

- *Done properly.*
- *The jobs that need doing.*
- *Maintenance, done properly.*

**Rules:**
- Keep the full stop. The line is a quiet statement, not a shout — the period does that work. No exclamation marks.
- Don't reword it ("Jobs done right", "We do it properly" etc.) — those are off-brand paraphrases. Use an approved variant or the full line.
- Sentence case, not Title Case. "The jobs that need doing, done properly." — not "The Jobs That Need Doing, Done Properly."
- The tagline is supporting copy, never part of the logo mark. Keep clear space between them.

## Logo

The EGO LTD mark is a **typographic lockup**, not a pictorial symbol. It has three parts:

1. **The badge** — a rounded square (radius matches the brand `0.75rem` family) containing a single bold **"E"**. On light surfaces the badge is near-black (`primary`) with a white "E". On dark surfaces it inverts: white badge, near-black "E".
2. **The wordmark** — **EGO LTD** set in the bold display face, tight tracking. "EGO" takes the foreground colour (near-black on light, white on dark); **"LTD" is tinted ember** — this is one of the very few approved standing uses of the accent, and it's what makes the mark unmistakably ours.
3. **The descriptor** — the word **MAINTENANCE** beneath the wordmark, small, uppercase, wide letter-spacing (~0.18em), in the muted/secondary colour. It sits quietly and labels what we do.

### Logo variants
- **Default (on light):** near-black badge + "E", foreground wordmark, ember "LTD", muted descriptor. Use on cream/white backgrounds.
- **Light (on dark):** white badge + near-black "E", white wordmark, ember "LTD", translucent-white descriptor. Use on dark photography, the dark app sidebar, dark hero panels.

### Clear space
Keep a minimum clear space around the whole lockup equal to **the height of the badge** (the "E" square) on every side. Nothing — text, photo edges, buttons, other logos — intrudes into that zone.

### Minimum size
Keep the badge at least **28px** tall on screen (its default app size is 36px / `h-9`). Below that the descriptor becomes unreadable — drop the descriptor before you drop legibility, or use the badge-only "E" as an app icon / favicon.

### Logo don'ts
- **Don't** recolour the badge or wordmark to arbitrary colours. The only colours in play are near-black, white, muted grey, and the ember "LTD".
- **Don't** make the whole wordmark ember. Ember is only ever the "LTD" (and the badge stays neutral). A fully-orange logo breaks the scarcity rule.
- **Don't** stretch, condense, skew, rotate, or add drop-shadows / glows / outlines to the mark.
- **Don't** rebuild the lockup in a different typeface or re-space it. Use the supplied asset.
- **Don't** put the default (dark) logo on a busy or dark photo where it loses contrast — switch to the light variant.
- **Don't** box the logo in its own container when it already sits on a clean surface, and don't add a tagline inside the clear-space zone.

## Colour Palette — Stone & Ember

The system is built on warm stone neutrals with **one** accent. The golden rule: **ember orange is scarce.** It is "the one thing that shouts" — reserved for the single most important action or highlight in any view. If everything is ember, nothing is. As a rule of thumb, ember should be well under ~10% of any layout.

Colours are defined as HSL channels (so alpha modifiers work) with hex equivalents for print and design tools.

### Core palette (light mode)

| Token | Role | Hex | HSL | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Background | Page / surface | `#F2EFE6` | `47 40% 95%` | Warm cream canvas. The page never sits on flat white — cards lift off this. |
| Foreground | Body text | `#1B1C18` | `75 8% 10%` | Near-black on-surface text. Default for all copy. |
| Card | Card surface | `#FFFFFF` | `0 0% 100%` | White cards that float above the cream background (the "lit-edge" cards). |
| Primary | Buttons / strong UI | `#171816` | `90 4% 9%` | Near-black. Quiet, premium. The **primary** does the structural work; the **accent** does the shouting. |
| Primary foreground | On primary | `#FFFFFF` | `0 0% 100%` | Text/icons on primary surfaces. |
| Secondary | Chips / quiet fills | `#E2E0D6` | `50 17% 86%` | Warm stone chip / secondary container. |
| Muted | Subtle surface | `#F0EEE7` | `47 23% 92%` | Section fills, table zebra, quiet panels. |
| Muted foreground | Secondary text | `#464742` | `72 4% 27%` | Captions, labels, metadata. |
| **Accent (Ember)** | **The one scarce accent** | **`#EF9F27`** | **`36 86% 55%`** | **CTAs, focus rings, key highlights, the ember "LTD". Use sparingly — one ember moment per view.** |
| Destructive | Errors / delete | `#BA1A1A` | `0 75% 42%` | Errors, destructive actions only. Not decorative. |
| Border | Hairlines | `#CDC9BD` | `45 13% 80%` | Warm hairline dividers, input borders. |
| Ring | Focus | `#EF9F27` | `36 86% 55%` | Ember keyboard-focus ring across the app. |

### Dark mode

Dark mode inverts the world into a near-black stone room. Crucially, **primary flips to light cream** so buttons stay visible, while **ember stays exactly as it is** — the accent is the one constant across both modes.

| Token | Role | Hex | HSL | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Background | Page | `#15150F`-ish | `75 8% 8%` | Deep near-black stone. |
| Card | Card surface | `#2C2C2A` | `60 2% 17%` | Raised card on the dark canvas. |
| Foreground | Text | `#F3F1EA` | `47 27% 94%` | Warm off-white text. |
| Primary | Buttons | `#F3F1EA` | `47 27% 94%` | **Flips to light** so CTAs stay legible on dark. |
| Accent (Ember) | Accent | `#EF9F27` | `36 86% 55%` | **Unchanged** — ember is constant. |
| Border | Hairlines | — | `72 5% 26%` | Low-contrast warm divider. |

> Note: the **sidebar / app chrome stays dark in both modes** — it's premium chrome. Dark sidebar, ember active-state, off-white labels, regardless of light/dark page.

### Chart palette

Charts pull from a fixed, on-brand sequence (in order): **ember `#EF9F27` → near-black `#171816` → warm grey `~#B0AC9F` → light ember `~#E0B97A` → slate `~#4D8AA8`**. Use them in this order so the most important series reads ember and the rest stay neutral. Don't introduce new chart colours.

### Ember scarcity — the one rule that matters most

- **One ember moment per screen.** The primary CTA, the active nav item, a single key stat, or a focus ring — pick one job for ember in any given view.
- Ember is for **action and attention**, never for large fills, backgrounds, or body text.
- Status colours (success green, warning, info, destructive red) are **functional**, not part of the expressive palette — use them only for their meaning.
- When in doubt, use **near-black primary** for emphasis and save ember for the genuine call-to-action.

## Typography

EGO LTD uses a **system sans-serif stack** — no custom webfont is loaded, which keeps pages fast and rendering native on every device. (The display intent is a tight, slightly condensed sans in the spirit of Barlow Semi Condensed; in production we render with the platform sans stack and tight tracking to get the same grounded, no-nonsense feel.)

- **UI / body:** system sans (`-apple-system, Segoe UI, Roboto, …`). Antialiased, slightly tight tracking (`-0.006em`) at body sizes.
- **Display / headings (h1–h3):** same stack, **bold**, with tightened tracking (`-0.005em`) for a confident, compact headline.
- **Numerals:** use **tabular figures** in tables, totals, IDs and stats so columns line up. This matters in invoices and ops tables.

### Type rules
- Headlines are **bold and tight**, sentence case. Avoid all-caps for long headings (reserve uppercase for short labels and the MAINTENANCE descriptor).
- Body copy is regular weight, comfortable line length, near-black on cream/white.
- Labels, eyebrows, and small descriptors use **uppercase + wide tracking** (~0.15–0.18em) in muted grey — the same treatment as the logo descriptor.
- Don't introduce new typefaces (no Google webfonts, no decorative display faces) without recording them in `docs/PULSE-UI-DESIGN-SYSTEM.md` first.

### Indicative type scale

| Level | Use | Weight | Tracking |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display / H1 | Page hero | Bold | -0.005em |
| H2 | Section heading | Bold | -0.005em |
| H3 | Sub-section / card title | Semibold–Bold | -0.005em |
| Body | Paragraphs, UI text | Regular | -0.006em |
| Small / caption | Metadata, helper text | Medium | normal |
| Label / eyebrow | Tags, descriptors | Medium, UPPERCASE | ~0.16em |

## Spacing, Radius & Surface Treatment

- **Corner radius:** the brand radius is **`0.75rem` (12px)** for cards, buttons, badges and inputs. Smaller controls step down proportionally (e.g. ~8px on compact buttons). Keep corners soft but not pill-round — grounded, not bubbly.
- **Spacing:** generous, consistent padding. Let cards breathe; don't crowd. White space is part of the premium feel.
- **The "lit edge" signature:** surfaces catch light on the top edge — a 1px hairline highlight plus a soft warm-tinted lift. White cards visibly float off the cream background. Repeat this on cards, modals and active nav. It's the visual signature: crop the logo and you should still know it's us. The one hero surface per screen gets a stronger **ember-tinted** edge.
- **The canvas:** pages carry a near-invisible ambient glow — warm ember light from the top-right, a cool stone counter-glow from the left. Never a flat fill. This gives every screen quiet depth.
- **Motion:** one easing curve (`cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`) and one base duration (~180ms), used everywhere. Surfaces lift gently on hover; nothing snaps. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Imagery & Photography

Photography must look like **real EGO work**, not a stock library. The whole point is trust, and stock undermines it instantly.

**Direction — do:**
- Real jobs, real EGO vans, real engineers (in branded workwear where possible).
- **Tidy worksites** — clean tools, dust sheets down, the "after" shot that proves we tidy up. Tidiness is a brand value made visible.
- Honest, well-lit, slightly warm photography that matches the stone-and-ember palette. South-East London settings — recognisable, local, grounded.
- Before/after pairs, hands-on detail shots, the finished result.
- People who look like the actual team and actual customers — natural, not posed.

**Direction — don't:**
- No generic stock "smiling man in hard hat with clipboard."
- No staged perfection that looks fake; no heavy filters, no cold blue grading, no oversaturation.
- No clutter, mess, or unsafe practice in frame (it contradicts "done properly").
- Don't drown photos in ember — if you tint, keep it subtle and warm.

**Treatment:** photos sit in cards with the same `0.75rem` radius and lit-edge. On dark hero panels, use the light logo variant and ensure text contrast.

## Iconography

- Use a **single, consistent line-icon set** (the app uses Lucide via the PULSE UI system). Don't mix icon families.
- Icons are **functional and quiet** — stroke icons in foreground/muted colour, not filled, not decorative.
- An icon may take ember **only** when it marks the single accent action in a view (e.g. the primary CTA's icon, an active state). Default icon colour is neutral.
- Keep icon weight consistent with the type around it; align to the grid; don't stretch or recolour arbitrarily.

## UI / App Brand Consistency (PULSE UI)

The web app, engineer PWA and customer portal are all built on the **PULSE UI** design system, themed to Stone & Ember. To stay consistent:

- **Reuse the primitives** in `app/src/components/ui/*`. Don't hand-roll bespoke buttons, inputs or cards.
- **Use semantic tokens** — `bg-primary`, `text-muted-foreground`, `bg-accent`, `ring`, etc. **Never raw hex** in components. Colour decisions live in the theme, not in markup.
- The full component spec, tokens, themes and chart rules live in **`docs/PULSE-UI-DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`** — read it before any UI change. The default app theme is the dark-blue glassmorphic variant for the workspace chrome, with the Stone & Ember light/dark world for content.
- Don't add fonts, colours, radii or shadows without recording them in that design-system doc first.
- The marketing landing site and the app must read as the same brand — same palette, same lit-edge cards, same logo, same voice.

## Accessibility & Contrast

- **Text contrast:** body and UI text is near-black on cream/white (light) or off-white on near-black (dark) — both clear WCAG AA comfortably. Status text tones are specifically tuned to pass AA on white cards.
- **Ember on white:** ember `#EF9F27` is a mid-tone — it does **not** pass AA for small body text on white. Use ember for **large elements, fills behind dark text, focus rings and icons**, not for small coloured text. Buttons use near-black text on ember or white text on near-black, never ember text on cream.
- **Focus is always visible:** a 2px ember focus ring with 2px offset on every interactive element. Never remove focus outlines.
- **Don't rely on colour alone:** pair status colour with an icon or label (e.g. a red "Overdue" tag with text, not just a red dot).
- **Motion:** all entrance animations, hover lifts and shimmers collapse under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Maintain the badge minimum size and clear space so the logo stays legible.

## Tone of Voice (pointer)

In a sentence: **plain, warm, British, and straight** — we talk like a skilled tradesperson you trust, not a corporation or a hard-sell ad. Clear over clever, concrete over vague, confident without bragging; we say what we'll do, do it, and tell you it's done. ## Brand Do / Don't Checklist

**Do**
- Keep ember scarce — one accent moment per view; near-black does the structural work.
- Write the name as **EGO LTD** in capitals; use **EGO LIMITED** only for legal contexts.
- Use the full tagline with its full stop, or an approved short variant.
- Float white cards off the warm cream canvas with the lit-edge signature.
- Use semantic tokens (`bg-primary`, `bg-accent`…), never raw hex, in the app.
- Use real, tidy job photography that looks like genuine EGO work.
- Keep the visible focus ring and respect reduced-motion.
- Use tabular figures in tables, totals and invoices.

**Don't**
- Don't flood layouts with ember or use it for body text / large fills.
- Don't recolour, stretch, glow, or rebuild the logo, or make the whole wordmark orange.
- Don't reword the tagline or add exclamation marks.
- Don't use stock imagery, cold grading, or messy/unsafe worksites.
- Don't introduce new fonts, colours, radii or shadows without recording them in the design-system doc.
- Don't put the dark logo on dark/busy backgrounds — switch to the light variant.
- Don't mix icon families or fill/recolour icons arbitrarily.
