## 1. Purpose & Scope

### 1.1 What this document is
This is the operating manual for EGO LTD. It defines how we take work in, price it, do it, bill it, and close it — to one consistent standard, every time, regardless of who is on the job. It exists so the business runs the same way whether the Founder is on site or not.

### 1.2 Who it applies to
Everyone who works for or on behalf of EGO LTD: Founder, Manager, Office staff, Engineers, and any subcontractor or specialist we engage.

### 1.3 Scope of our work
We deliver three pillars of service:
1. **Reactive repairs** — things have broken or gone wrong; most are booked within the same week.
2. **Planned upkeep** — scheduled, recurring maintenance.
3. **The small jobs** — half-day and odd jobs that other firms won't book.

Our trade catalogue: Carpentry & Joinery; Painting & Decorating; Minor (non-gas) Plumbing; Walls & Tiling; Exterior & Grounds; Mounting & Fitting; Property Turnarounds (void works); Planned Maintenance contracts.

### 1.4 The hard boundary — READ THIS FIRST
**EGO LTD does NO gas work and NO notifiable electrical work in-house. Ever.**
- Gas work is carried out **only** by Gas Safe registered engineers.
- Notifiable electrical work is carried out **only** by qualified electricians.
- Where a job needs either, EGO arranges or recommends a certified specialist (Section 9).
- **We never cut corners on this. There is no exception, no "quick favour", no "while I'm here".** If in doubt, stop and escalate.

### 1.5 How to use it
Find the section for the task in front of you, follow the numbered steps, and complete the relevant checklist (Section 17). The EGO app is the single source of truth — if it isn't recorded in the app, it didn't happen.

## 2. Company Values & Service Standards

Our promise: **do the jobs that need doing, the way they should be done, including the ones nobody else wants.** Every person represents EGO. The four non-negotiable standards:

1. **Turn up in the window.** We commit to an arrival window and we keep it. If we're going to be late, the customer hears it from us *before* the window closes — not after.
2. **Fixed or clear price first.** The customer knows what it will cost, in writing, before we start. No surprises on the invoice.
3. **Tidy up.** We leave every site cleaner than a reasonable customer would expect. Dust sheets down, debris removed, surfaces wiped.
4. **Honest about limits.** If it's outside our trade, beyond safe scope, or needs a certified specialist, we say so plainly and arrange the right person. We never bluff a job.

Underpinning behaviours: polite, on time, presentable, ID visible, respectful of the customer's home or premises, and straight-talking about what we found and what we did.

## 4. Enquiry Handling & Lead Intake

Every enquiry, by every channel, ends up as a customer + enquiry record in the EGO app. Nothing lives only in someone's head or inbox.

### 4.1 Response-time targets
- **Phone:** answer within the working day; if missed, call back within 2 working hours.
- **Email / website "Get a quote":** acknowledge within 4 working hours; substantive reply (quote or booked visit) within 1 working day.
- **Inbound email (IMAP inbox):** triaged at least twice daily — first thing and mid-afternoon.

### 4.2 Phone enquiry (Office)
1. Answer: "EGO Limited, how can I help?"
2. Capture: name, address (with postcode), phone, email, sector (homeowner / landlord / agent / commercial).
3. Describe the problem in the customer's words; note urgency and access constraints.
4. Set expectation: same-week for reactive where possible; flag if it may need a specialist (gas/electrical).
5. Create the **customer** (search first to avoid duplicates) and the **enquiry/job** in the app.
6. Confirm next step and timescale before ending the call.

### 4.3 Website "Get a quote" / inbound email (Office)
1. Read the enquiry in the **inbox** module. Use **AI draft assist** to draft an acknowledgement if helpful — but a human reads, edits, and sends. AI never sends on its own.
2. Match to an existing customer or create a new one.
3. If detail is thin, reply with 3–4 specific questions (what, where, how long, photos if possible).
4. Triage the trade and likely pillar (reactive / planned / small job). Flag the gas/electrical boundary immediately if relevant.
5. Convert to a job record once scope is clear enough to quote or book a survey.

### 4.4 Triage decision rules
- **Inside our catalogue + clear scope** → quote or book.
- **Needs a site visit to scope** → book a survey slot.
- **Needs gas / notifiable electrical** → log it, tell the customer plainly, route to specialist engagement (Section 9). Do not promise to do it in-house.
- **Emergency / risk to safety** → Section 16.

## 5. Quoting & Estimating

**Rule above all else: the price is agreed in writing BEFORE any work starts.** No written agreement, no work.

### 5.1 Pricing model
1. **Quote per job** wherever the scope is clear — this is the default and the customer's preference.
2. Where a fixed quote isn't possible, use rate-based pricing:
   - **Call-out / first hour** (covers attendance + first hour).
   - **Hourly thereafter**, billed in **30-minute blocks** after the first hour.
   - **Half-day** = up to 4 hours.
   - **Full day** = up to 8 hours.
3. **Materials at cost, itemised.** We pass materials through at cost and list them line by line. (See Section 10.)

### 5.2 When to use which
- **Fixed/per-job:** defined deliverable (hang a door, tile a splashback, paint a room, turnaround a void). Preferred — gives the customer certainty.
- **Hourly:** investigative, "see what we find" work, or many small unrelated tasks in one visit.
- **Half/full day:** a known block of work where exact tasks may flex but the time is the unit sold.

### 5.3 Building and sending the quote (Office, approved per Section 3)
1. In the job record, build the quote: scope of works (plainly written), price basis, materials estimate, exclusions, and assumptions (access, parking, condition).
2. State clearly anything that would change the price (e.g. "subject to no hidden rot behind panel").
3. Note explicitly where a certified specialist will be needed and how that's handled.
4. Large or non-standard quotes: **Manager reviews; Founder approves** before it goes out.
5. AI draft assist may help word the quote; **AI never prices and never sends.** A human sets the numbers and clicks send.
6. Send via the app. Record the sent quote against the job.
7. Follow up if no response within a sensible window (typically 3–5 working days).

### 5.4 Acceptance
- Quote accepted → record acceptance against the job, then proceed to scheduling (Section 6).
- Any change in scope on site → **stop, re-quote, get written agreement** before continuing. Variations are never assumed.

## 6. Job Creation & Scheduling

### 6.1 Create the job record (Office)
1. Confirm the customer exists and details are correct.
2. Create the **job**: title, full scope, agreed price basis, site address, access notes, materials needed, and the linked accepted quote.
3. Attach any photos or documents from the enquiry.
4. Set status — the job moves through: **enquiry → customer → job → schedule → on-site → complete → invoice → payment → closed.** Use the app's status; don't skip stages.

### 6.2 Schedule and assign (Manager)
1. Open the **schedule / calendar**. Check engineer availability and skills against the job's trade.
2. Assign the right engineer (or a specialist via Section 9 if gas/electrical).
3. Set a realistic **arrival window** and expected duration. Build in travel time across London.
4. **Avoid clashes:** the calendar must show no overlapping assignments for the same engineer; allow buffer for over-runs and travel.
5. Confirm the appointment to the customer with the window and the tracking link (Section 12).

### 6.3 Recurring & planned jobs
1. For planned upkeep and maintenance contracts, set up a **recurring plan** so the app generates jobs on schedule automatically.
2. Each generated occurrence is a normal job — scheduled, delivered, logged, and (per contract) billed.
3. Review the recurring schedule monthly so seasonal work (gutters, exterior) lands at the right time.

### 6.4 Reschedules & cancellations
- Customer-initiated: re-book in the calendar, confirm new window, keep the same job record.
- EGO-initiated (illness, weather, over-run): notify the customer **as early as possible**, offer the next available window, log the reason in the activity log.

## 7. On-Site Procedure (Engineers)

The engineer works through the **mobile PWA** (Today / My Jobs / job field view). Everything below is recorded in the app as it happens.

### 7.1 Before leaving
1. Log in to the PWA. **Engineer login is protected by 2FA (TOTP)** — keep your authenticator to hand.
2. Review **Today** and your assigned jobs. Read the scope, access notes, and agreed price basis.
3. Confirm you have the materials, tools, and PPE for the job.
4. If offline coverage is likely, note the app's offline indicator — the field view works offline and syncs later.

### 7.2 Arrival (in the window)
1. Arrive within the committed window. If running late, the **customer hears from us before the window closes** — call or message via the app.
2. Introduce yourself, show ID, confirm the job and scope with the customer.
3. Park considerately and legally.

### 7.3 Before-work checks
1. Walk the work area with the customer. Confirm scope matches what was quoted.
2. **Spot any new or hidden issue, or anything outside scope → STOP.** Do not proceed on the extra. Contact Office/Manager to re-quote and get written agreement first (Section 5.4).
3. Identify hazards (Section 8). If RAMS is required for the task, confirm it's in place before starting.
4. Lay dust sheets / protection. Photograph the **before** state.

### 7.4 The work
1. Do the job to standard — the way it should be done, not the fastest shortcut.
2. **Never** undertake gas or notifiable electrical work. If the job turns out to need it, stop and escalate (Sections 8.6, 9).
3. Keep the customer informed of progress and any decisions.
4. Use the right materials; keep receipts/records for anything bought on the day.

### 7.5 Photos, notes, checklist
1. Take clear **during** and **after** photos in the field view.
2. Write concise notes: what was found, what was done, what was used, anything the customer should know, and any recommended follow-up.
3. Complete the job **checklist** in the app.

### 7.6 Customer sign-off
1. Walk the completed work with the customer.
2. Capture the **customer sign-off signature** in the PWA.
3. If the customer isn't present, note that and record remote confirmation where possible.

### 7.7 Leave it tidy
1. Remove all debris and waste. Dispose of it responsibly.
2. Wipe down surfaces; remove dust sheets; return furniture.
3. Final walk-through with the customer; confirm satisfaction.
4. Mark the job **complete** in the app. This is the trigger for invoicing (Section 11).

## 8. Health & Safety + Compliance

EGO works safely and legally on every site. Compliance records live in the app's **compliance** module (certificates, incident reports, RAMS).

### 8.1 RAMS (Risk Assessments & Method Statements)
- Required for: work at height, void/turnaround works, commercial/facilities sites that mandate it, and any task with non-trivial risk.
- Manager produces/approves the RAMS in the app before the job; the engineer reads and works to it.
- Simple low-risk domestic jobs use a documented dynamic risk assessment on arrival (note hazards in job notes).

### 8.2 PPE
- Minimum on site: safety footwear, eye protection where cutting/drilling, gloves as appropriate, dust mask where dust is generated.
- Task-specific PPE as the RAMS dictates. No correct PPE → don't start that task.

### 8.3 Working at height
- Use the right access equipment (steps, podium, tower) in good condition. Inspect before use.
- No improvised platforms. If proper access can't be made safe, stop and escalate.

### 8.4 Asbestos awareness
- Pre-2000 properties may contain asbestos. **Do not** drill, cut, or disturb suspect materials (textured coatings, old tiles, insulation board, soffits).
- Suspect asbestos → **stop work, isolate the area, photograph, log it, and escalate to Manager.** Specialist survey/removal is arranged before work continues.

### 8.5 Incident reporting
1. Make the area safe and attend to anyone hurt first.
2. Log an **incident report** in the compliance module **the same day**: date/time, location, people involved, what happened, injuries/damage, immediate action, photos.
3. Notify Manager immediately; Founder for any serious incident.
4. Manager reviews for root cause and corrective action; record the outcome.

### 8.6 The no-gas / no-electrical boundary (escalation)
- Discover the job needs gas or notifiable electrical work → **stop that element.**
- Explain to the customer plainly that EGO arranges a certified specialist (Gas Safe / qualified electrician).
- Log it on the job and hand to Manager/Founder for specialist engagement (Section 9).
- Never attempt it, never "make it safe" beyond genuine emergency isolation, never advise the customer to DIY it.

### 8.7 Certificates
- Where a specialist issues a certificate (gas safety, electrical), the certificate is uploaded to the **compliance** module against the job/asset and shared with the customer.

## 9. Subcontractor & Specialist Engagement

We bring in certified specialists for anything outside our in-house remit — first and foremost gas and notifiable electrical.

### 9.1 When to engage
- **Always** for gas (Gas Safe registered engineer) and notifiable electrical (qualified electrician).
- For trades/volume beyond current capacity, where it serves the customer better.

### 9.2 Vetting (before first engagement — Manager/Founder)
1. Confirm relevant qualification/registration (e.g. **Gas Safe number**, electrician's competent-person scheme).
2. Obtain and record proof of **public liability insurance** (and confirm it's current).
3. Confirm references / track record.
4. Store all documents in **procurement → subcontractors** with expiry dates flagged for re-check.

### 9.3 Engagement & PO process
1. Define the scope and agree the specialist's price.
2. Raise a **purchase order** in the procurement module against the job.
3. Brief the specialist on access, site rules, and any RAMS.
4. On completion, collect their **certificate** and upload to compliance.
5. Match the specialist's invoice to the PO before payment.

### 9.4 Customer-facing
- We tell the customer who is doing the specialist work and that they're certified. We remain the point of contact and coordinate the job.

## 10. Materials & Procurement

Handled in the **procurement** module (suppliers, parts, purchase orders, stock movements).

### 10.1 Principles
- **Materials are charged at cost and itemised** on the invoice. We do not hide a markup in materials.
- Our margin is in our labour rates and quotes, not in inflating parts.
- Keep **every receipt**; attach to the job.

### 10.2 Purchase orders
1. For anticipated materials, raise a **PO** against the job before purchase.
2. Buy from approved suppliers where possible.
3. Record stock movements when drawing from or returning to van/store stock.

### 10.3 On-the-day purchases (Engineers)
1. Buy only what the job needs.
2. Photograph/keep the receipt and log it against the job in the field view so Office can itemise it accurately.

### 10.4 Stock
- Maintain a sensible van/store stock of common consumables; record movements so reorder points are visible.

## 11. Invoicing & Payment

**Payment is by bank transfer only — there is no card gateway.** Invoices are sequential and VAT-ready.

### 11.1 Generate the invoice (Office)
1. Triggered by a job marked **complete** with sign-off captured.
2. Generate the invoice **from the completed job** so labour, agreed price, and itemised materials carry through.
3. Confirm VAT is applied correctly and totals are right.
4. The invoice takes the **next sequential number — no gaps.** Each invoice/job carries a **unique payment reference.**
5. AI draft assist may help with wording; **AI never prices and never marks anything paid.** A human checks and sends.

### 11.2 Send
1. Send via the app to the customer's email.
2. The invoice clearly shows: line items, materials at cost, VAT, total, **bank transfer details**, and the **unique payment reference** the customer must quote.

### 11.3 Reconciliation (Office/Manager)
1. Match incoming bank transfers to invoices by payment reference.
2. Mark the invoice **paid** in the **payments** module (a human action — never automated by AI).
3. Move the job to **closed** once paid.

### 11.4 Chasing overdue
1. **Reminder 1** — at due date: polite "just a nudge", invoice + reference re-attached.
2. **Reminder 2** — 7 days overdue: firmer, restate reference and bank details.
3. **Reminder 3** — 14 days overdue: escalate to Manager; phone call follows the email.
4. **21+ days** — Founder decides on further action.
- All reminders logged in the activity log. Automated reminders may be configured, but a human owns the escalation decisions.

## 14. Data Protection & GDPR

EGO holds customer personal data and property/job information. We handle it lawfully and minimally.

### 14.1 Lawful basis
- **Contract** (delivering the work the customer asked for) and **legitimate interests** (running the business, chasing payment). Marketing emails/campaigns require **consent** — subscribers opt in and can opt out.

### 14.2 Data minimisation & retention
- Collect only what the job needs.
- Retain records as long as needed for the work, warranty, tax/VAT, and legal obligation — then dispose.

### 14.3 Soft-delete & secure storage
- Deletions are **soft-deletes** in the app — recoverable and auditable, not hard-wiped on a whim.
- **Job photos are stored securely** and used only for the job, records, and (with care) demonstrating work. Don't share customer property images publicly without consent.

### 14.4 Subject access / data requests
1. A customer request to see, correct, or erase their data goes to Manager/Founder.
2. Verify identity. Respond within the statutory timescale.
3. Erasure requests are balanced against records we must keep for tax/legal reasons — explain what's kept and why.

### 14.5 Secrets handling
- Application secrets and credentials are **never** committed to the code repository — they live only in server-side configuration. Staff do not share logins. Engineer accounts use **2FA (TOTP)**.

## 15. Quality Assurance & Complaints

### 15.1 Snagging
- Engineer self-checks against the job checklist before sign-off. Anything not right is fixed before leaving.

### 15.2 Callbacks — redo vs new job (decision rule)
- **Redo (no charge):** the issue is our defective work or something we should have done within the agreed scope. We return promptly and put it right at our cost.
- **New job (chargeable):** the customer wants additional/different work, or the fault is unrelated to what we did (new fault, wear, third-party damage). Quote it as a new job per Section 5.
- **Borderline → Manager decides.** Bias towards goodwill; protect the relationship and the brand.

### 15.3 Complaint log
- Every complaint is logged (Section 17 fields), assigned an owner, and tracked to resolution. Manager reviews patterns monthly; repeat issues drive a fix to process or training.

## 16. Emergency / Out-of-Hours Procedure

Standard hours are Mon–Fri 8am–6pm; **out-of-hours is by arrangement.**

### 16.1 Safety first
- If anyone is in danger, or there's risk of fire/gas/flood/electrical danger → advise the customer to call **999** and the relevant utility's **emergency line** (e.g. gas leak → National Gas Emergency Service 0800 111 999). EGO does not attend life-safety emergencies as first responder.

### 16.2 Triage an out-of-hours call (Office/Manager on call)
1. Establish what's happening and whether it's a genuine emergency or can wait to the next working day.
2. **Make-safe only:** isolate water at the stopcock, contain a leak, secure a broken door/window. We do not undertake gas or notifiable electrical work out-of-hours either.
3. If it needs a gas/electrical specialist, route to one (Section 9) — we coordinate, we don't improvise.

### 16.3 Attendance
1. Agree the out-of-hours basis and any premium **before** attending (Section 5 — price in writing still applies; a quick written message confirming the rate suffices).
2. Create/raise the job in the app even if logged after the fact.
3. Make safe, photograph, log, and book proper remedial work for normal hours.

### 16.4 Escalation
- On-call Office → Manager → Founder. Anything involving injury, major damage, or media/reputational risk goes to the Founder immediately.
