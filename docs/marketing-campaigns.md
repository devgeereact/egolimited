# EGO LIMITED — Marketing Campaigns

Ready-to-send email campaigns for the in-app **Campaigns** tool. Three audiences:
**Commercial contracts**, **Residential jobs**, **Maintenance plans**. Email-only
(no SMS — M10 dropped). Written in the EGO brand voice (plain, straight, proof-led,
British; £ no space; max one "!"; no jargon, no em dashes).

**Contact block for every email:** EGO LTD · 07539 705576 · hello@egolimited.com ·
egolimited.com. Brand line: *"The jobs that need doing, done properly."*

**How to use:** Campaigns → new campaign → pick the audience → paste subject + body.
For the header image, click *Generate image* and paste the **Image prompt** given under
each email (the app now prepends the EGO brand style automatically, so keep prompts to
the scene only — it stays on-brand, no text baked in).

Each email keeps AIDA: attention (subject + first line), interest (proof), desire (the
offer), action (one clear CTA). Send business-hours, Tue–Thu, 8–10am works best.

## Notes on brand + images
- The app prepends the **EGO brand image style** to every generated image (palette,
  real UK settings, and a hard "no text/logos" rule so nothing renders as gibberish).
  Keep image prompts to the **scene only** — don't ask for text on the image; add the
  headline as email copy instead.
- Owner can tune the image look in **Settings → Integrations** via key
  `brand_image_style`, and the writing style via `brand_voice`.
- Every campaign email is recorded to **Sent** and to the subscriber's history.
